Delete your CampusBanter account

You can delete your account and data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open CampusBanter and go to the Campus ID tab.
  2. Tap Manage, then Delete Account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm with Delete Forever.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email Support.ProjectZ@gmail.com from the college email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my CampusBanter account". We will delete your account within 15 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email and password) and your profile, including your anonymous alias and public encryption key.
  • Your posts and comments.
  • Your votes, saved posts, blocked-users list and notifications.

What may remain

  • Encrypted messages you sent may remain in the other person's conversation as unreadable ciphertext. They can no longer be read once your keys are gone.
  • Reports you filed, or that were filed about content, may be kept for a limited time for safety and legal reasons.
  • Copies in backups are removed within a short period as backups roll over.
