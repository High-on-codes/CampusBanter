Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026

This policy explains what CampusBanter (the "App", operated by the CampusBanter team, "we" or "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. CampusBanter is built to keep you anonymous to other students; we collect only what is needed to run the service.

In short: we use your university email only to verify you're a student. Other students never see it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted and we cannot read them.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or precise device identifiers, and we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. How anonymity works

  • Your posts and comments show an alias, not your name or email. Each post can use a different alias.
  • Internally, each post and comment carries your account ID so we can enable replies, blocking, deletion and moderation. Other users cannot see the link between your alias and your email.
  • If you post as a "Verified Student", the name and branch you entered in your profile are shown publicly on that post.
  • Moderators can see reported and flagged posts and the account ID that wrote them. They cannot read direct messages.

3. How we use information

To operate and secure the App, verify eligibility, show content to the right campus audience, deliver messages, enforce our Terms, investigate abuse, comply with the law, and fix problems. We do not use your data for advertising or to build marketing profiles.

4. Sharing

  • Service provider: Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) processes and stores data on our behalf in Google Cloud data centres. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Other students: your public content, alias and campus label; nothing else.
  • Legal and safety: we may disclose information (including the email linked to an account) if required by law or a valid legal request, to enforce our Terms, or to prevent serious harm. We cannot disclose message contents because we cannot read them.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by Google. Access to moderation tools is restricted to authorised administrators. Direct messages use end-to-end encryption (AES-256-GCM with keys derived on the participants' devices). No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data while your account is active.
  • Delete your account anytime in the App (Campus ID → Manage → Delete Account). This deletes your profile, posts, comments, votes, saved items, blocks and notifications and removes your authentication record.
  • Encrypted messages you sent may remain in the other person's conversation, unreadable once your key is deleted. Reports you filed may be kept for safety records. Residual copies in backups are removed on a rolling basis.
  • Disappearing messages are hidden from the App after 24 hours. The encrypted data may remain on our servers until the account is deleted.

7. Your rights and choices

You can access and edit your profile in the App, delete your account, block users, and report content. Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR) you may also have the right to access, correct, erase or export your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a regulator. To exercise these rights, email Support.ProjectZ@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and, in any event, within the period required by law.

8. Children

CampusBanter is for adults at colleges and universities and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from people under that age. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Google may process data in data centres outside your country. We rely on Google's safeguards for such transfers.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and, for material changes, notify you in the App or by email.
